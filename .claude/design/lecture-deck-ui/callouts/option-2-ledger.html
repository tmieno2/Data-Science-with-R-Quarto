Ledger — a named chip on every block

The chip names the kind of block in words rather than leaving it to a colour, which is the failure mode of the current icons-off callouts. Square corners and filled labels tie it to the title band. It reads harder from the back of a room than Chalkline and costs ~18px more per block — the trade is explicitness against vertical space.
